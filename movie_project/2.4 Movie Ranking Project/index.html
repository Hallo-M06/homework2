Matthew's Top Movies

Top 6 animated movies of all-time

Spiderman: Into the Spiderverse

A masterpiece in animation that revolutionized the industry and told a great and compeling stories using an incredibly iconic character.

The Mitchells vs. the Machines

A very relatable and funny film focusing on a family facing a robot apocalypse. With endearing characters, stylish animation, and incredible comedic bits, what's not to love.

The Lion King

A classic for a reason. It has great and memorable songs as well as a clever spin on the timeless tale of Hamlet.

Wall-E

A beautiful and heartfelt story through space while also being a cautionary tale on the dangers of overconsumption. A lovely little feature from Pixar.

The Wild Robot

An emotional journey through a robot's struggles in nature and trials in motherhood. It has incredible music, amazing characters and gorgeous animation.

The Lego Movie

A hilarious time with a suprising amount of heart. A true celebration of all things Lego with creativity abundant in all aspects of the film.
